- Drift: **two findings**. F6 — the SKU's headline feature (FR-045 auto-poster) has no implementation path; F5 — FR-046's pledge engine as written violates the spec's own money-state wall (FR-003/SC-005). F2/F4 additionally make the SKU's auth story for external B2B customers (self-hosted, non-ecosystem deployments per business plan §1) undeliverable as specced — the Directus-IdP-or-nothing rule (FR-005) needs the FR-013 pluggable-auth seam to become the escape route, or the SKU text must scope to ecosystem-internal deployments.

## Alternative approaches considered

- **Local auth adapter for external deployments** (F6/F4 adjacent): FR-013 already demands swappable Auth — the cheapest resolution of the business-plan-vs-FR-005 tension is two auth adapters (Payload-native for $149 customers, Directus for ecosystem instances) rather than weakening the IdP mandate ecosystem-wide.
- **"undrlla-ecosystem" single-audience model** (F2): simpler than per-service `aud` minting, at the cost of token portability across ecosystem services. A deliberate trade for the lead, not a silent drift.
- **IdP webhook push instead of login-time re-sync** (F11): one webhook from Directus (profile/deletion events) fixes both staleness and dead tombstones; alternative is a periodic sweep — either beats the current login-only model.
